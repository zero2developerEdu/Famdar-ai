Famdar.Ai — Photo Tag Tool

Add a camera-style location/date tag to photos manually or with device GPS.

GPS and address retrieval require browser permission and internet. The photo is processed in your browser; it is not uploaded to a server.

Choose a photo to preview the tag.
Tag bottom-left, badge top-right, compass bottom-right.

The tag preview updates as you edit the fields. Download exports the image with the tag burned in.
